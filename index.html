AI policy

Honest about how it's made

Amazon KDP calls an image AI-generated when an AI tool creates it, even after heavy editing, and authors must declare AI-generated cover images when they publish. AI-assisted work, where a person creates the image and AI only helps refine it, doesn't need declaring.

I tell every client in writing which category their cover falls into, and I never describe AI-generated art as merely AI-assisted. Prefer no AI? I'll use licensed stock, original photography or typography only.
